The surface ramp, border, status colours (success, warning, danger, info) and the highlight colour are our choice. The logo has no green, amber or red, so those four are picked for contrast, not sampled.

Tokens

  • Surfaces 0 to 3, border, strong border, text, muted text, accent, accent-2, node, accent-ink, accent-soft, glow.
  • Status: success, warning, danger, info.
  • Type scale: 12, 13, 15, 18, 24, 32 px as --ui-text-xs to --ui-text-2xl. System font stack only. A monospace stack for ids, paths and code. Arabic falls through to the system Arabic face.
  • Spacing: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48 px.
  • Radius: 6, 10, 16 px and a pill.
  • Shadow: three levels. Glow: a 1 px ring plus a soft halo in the accent colour. Focus ring: two rings.
  • Motion: 120, 200 and 320 ms. Three easings: standard, out and a small spring. Under prefers-reduced-motion the three durations collapse to 0.01 ms.
  • Touch target: 40 px.

Themes

Dark is the base because the logo is dark. Light is a full alternative. The default is prefers-color-scheme. The header toggle sets Auto, Dark or Light and stores it in localStorage under aletheia.theme. The automatic light block and the manual light block must match; a test checks that.

Brand details as built

  • The brain mark (17 nodes, 34 lines) is an inline SVG in the header. As the loader its nodes pulse one after another; it stops under reduced motion and while the tab is hidden.
  • The tab icon is an SVG data URL in three states: idle (cyan), working (teal with a dot) and error (red with a dot). It follows the status bar.
  • Empty states use the faded brain with a small symbol (plus, magnifier, check, cross, rules). All colours are CSS variables, so both themes work.

Accessibility audit

Contrast is in the table above and enforced by test/ui-contrast.test.mjs. test/ui-a11y.test.mjs checks names, roles, ids, tab order, directions and the stylesheet rules on every page. A Chromium pass over 15 routes at 375, 768, 1280 and 1920 px (60 combinations, with a 200-character unbroken token and an Arabic paragraph in the data) found no page that scrolls sideways, no control without a name and no duplicate id, after the fixes listed in T-214. The two remaining targets under 40 px are disabled Markdown task-list checkboxes and checkboxes inside a 40 px label.

A second pass on 2026-10-27 in Playwright Chromium (150-row demo seed) loaded six routes — Work, a request detail, Rules, Suggested, Economics and Memories — at 375 and 1280 px in light and dark. On every combination document.documentElement.scrollWidth was at most innerWidth (the only overflow was the status strip's own horizontal scroll), and the console had no errors. This checked layout, not a keyboard walk; the keyboard pass is still the 2026-10-26 one above.
