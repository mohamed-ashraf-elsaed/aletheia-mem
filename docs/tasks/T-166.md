## Goal

Each request shows a progress meter and, when the data supports it, an ETA. Both come from stored rows, show their
evidence ids, and never invent a number. ETA is `null` unless there is a measured pace.

## Files to touch

- `src/hub/requests.mjs`
- `test/requests-progress.test.mjs` (new)

## What changed

- `sessionGroups` now records per session whether it has a session-end event, its summary memories, and its fact ids.
- `buildRequest` computes `progress` as the fraction of sessions that ended or produced a summary, capped at 99 unless
  the request is `Shipped` (then 100). `progressEvidence` lists the completed session ids, fact ids and summary ids.
- `eta` is reported only when the request is not shipped, has at least two sessions with distinct starts, has at least
  one completed session, and has remaining sessions. The pace is the mean gap between consecutive session starts.
  Otherwise `eta` and `etaEvidence` are `null`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A shipped request is 100% with no ETA.
- [x] One of three completed sessions gives 33% and a paced ETA with evidence.
- [x] One session, or no completed session, has no ETA.
- [x] A summary marks a session complete and feeds progress evidence.
