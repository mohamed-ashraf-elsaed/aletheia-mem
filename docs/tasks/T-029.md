## Goal

Merge a duplicate into the older id, union facts/files, keep the higher importance, and record the loser.

## Why

D4 section 23.5.6. See [20-claude-mem-feature-matrix.md](../20-claude-mem-feature-matrix.md) and, where relevant,
[22-improve-the-numbers.md](../22-improve-the-numbers.md) or [23-memory-building.md](../23-memory-building.md).

## Files to touch

- `src/hub/store.mjs`
- `test/merge-duplicates.test.mjs`

## Steps

1. Write the merge in one transaction.
2. Append the newer narrative under a 'Merged from' line.
3. Add a `duplicate_of` link and an audit row.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The older id survives.
- [ ] Facts and files are unioned.
- [ ] The audit row names both ids.

`store.mergeObservations` runs one transaction: the older row keeps its id, the newer narrative is appended under
`Merged from #<id>:`, importance and pin take the higher value, linked facts and observation keys move to the
survivor, the loser is kept with `status='merged'`, `memory_links(relation='duplicate_of')` is written, and the audit
row names both ids. A repeated merge does not append the same text twice.
