## Goal

Decouple capture from extraction with a queue that survives a restart and processes oldest-first.

## Why

D1 F-M01, F-M12; D4 section 23.7. See [20-claude-mem-feature-matrix.md](../20-claude-mem-feature-matrix.md) and, where relevant,
[22-improve-the-numbers.md](../22-improve-the-numbers.md) or [23-memory-building.md](../23-memory-building.md).

## Files to touch

- `src/hub/queue.mjs`
- `src/server.mjs`
- `test/queue.test.mjs`

## Steps

1. Add a `jobs` table with type, payload, attempts, next_attempt, status.
2. Claim one job at a time with a transaction.
3. Retry with exponential backoff and a max attempt count.
4. Resume pending jobs on startup.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A killed process resumes pending jobs.
- [ ] A failing job retries and then marks failed.
- [ ] Capture never blocks on the queue.

```bash
bun test test/queue.test.mjs
```
