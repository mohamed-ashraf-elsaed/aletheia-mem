# T-219: Add the goals roll-up panel to the Work tab

Status: validated
Phase: P6
Depends on: T-168, T-218
Validated on: 2026-10-27
Evidence:

## Goal

Replace the old paged goal list with the T-168 roll-up: each goal shows its status and aggregate progress, the
requests that serve it, and what came out of it. The open asks split into "being worked" and "nothing working", and
requests that serve no goal are counted in their own bucket.

## Files to touch

- `public/tabs/work.js`
- `public/tabs/work-goals.js` (new)
- `test/ui-work-goals-rollup.test.mjs` (new)
- `test/ui-work.test.mjs` (update)

## What changed

- `GET /v1/work/goals` is read through `pagedList` (it answers `total`, `limit`, `offset` and `hasMore`).
- A roll-up strip shows the goal count, open asks, being worked, nothing working and the no-goal count, plus the mean
  `aggregateProgress` of the goals on the page, labelled estimated.
- A goal card shows its status (Moving, Stalled, Nothing on it), aggregate progress with the request count, its
  linked requests with links to `#/work/request/<id>`, and its merged `whatCameOutOfIt`.
- `noGoal.requests` and `openAsks.beingWorked` / `nothingWorking` are listed with links.
- Pin, Unpin, Dismiss and Restore still post to `/api/hub/goals/:id` (T-144) through the optimistic helper. That old
  route keeps working; the goal list itself is not written.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] The panel shows the roll-up, the no-goal bucket and the two open-ask lists.
- [x] A goal shows its status, aggregate progress and linked requests.
- [x] Pin and dismiss remain optimistic and reversible.
- [x] `/api/hub/goals/:id` is still used for goal actions.
