# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in this project to be free of harassment for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be direct about the work and respectful to the person.
- Give and accept technical criticism without personal attacks.
- Do not publish another person's private information, transcripts, tokens or paths.
- Do not use the issue tracker to advertise, to buy or sell anything, or to submit generated text without checking it.
- Assume a claim needs a source. "It should work" is not a test result.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing private material without permission.
- Sustained disruption of issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Retaliation for reporting a problem in good faith.

## Scope

This applies in the repository, its issues and pull requests, and any space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report a problem to the maintainer through GitHub: <https://github.com/mohamed-ashraf-elsaed>. No email address is
published for this project. If the report needs a confidential channel, use GitHub's private reporting or its abuse
flow; do not put the details in a public issue.

The maintainer will read the report, keep it private where possible, and may warn, remove a comment, lock a thread,
or block an account. A report about the maintainer can be sent through GitHub's own abuse flow.
